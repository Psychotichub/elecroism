Key engineering concept

A BMS-controlled panel is built on 3 layers:

1. Power layer

→ ACB, MCCB, busbars, protection

2. Control layer

→ relays, contactors, coils

3. Intelligence layer

→ BMS (monitoring + automation)

Final takeaway

An electrical panel with BMS is not just “switching devices”—it is a hierarchical system:

Protection → MCCB/ACB
Switching → contactors/relays
Control → coils + interposing relays
Intelligence → BMS communication modules
